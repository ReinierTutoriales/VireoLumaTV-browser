VireoLumaTV is a browser for Android TV, maintained by ReinierTutoriales.

Use the remote to browse, switch tabs, save pages and manage downloads. The address field offers local suggestions from bookmarks and history. Settings include per-site blocking, search providers, browser identification and remote shortcuts.

Pages run in the Android system WebView. Filter lists are fetched from their configured providers. Private mode excludes visits from normal history; downloaded files and saved bookmarks remain on the device.

Source and support: https://github.com/ReinierTutoriales/VireoLumaTV-browser
